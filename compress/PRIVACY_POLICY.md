# Privacy Policy / 隐私权政策

**Effective Date / 生效日期: November 2, 2026**

## English Version

### Introduction

Marmot Compress ("we", "our", or "the App") is a privacy-first image and video compression application. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it on your mobile device.

**The short version: Marmot Compress does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data. Every image and video you compress stays on your device and never leaves it.**

### Information We Collect

**We do not collect any personal information.** Marmot Compress:

- Does **not** require an account or registration
- Does **not** collect, store, or process your photos or videos on any server
- Does **not** send any data over the internet
- Does **not** include advertisements, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs
- Does **not** sell or share data with third parties

### How the App Uses Device Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Photos / Media** (Android 13+) | Selecting images and videos for compression, and saving the processed results back to your gallery |
| **Storage** (Android 12 and below) | Reading and writing image/video files on older Android versions |

All permissions are used only to provide the App's compression and conversion features. You may deny any permission, and the App will simply not perform the related function.

### Data Storage and Security

- All image and video processing is performed **locally on your device**.
- Compressed files and intermediate results are stored **only in the App's private cache directory** on your device.
- Your original files are **not** modified unless you explicitly choose to overwrite them.
- No media files are uploaded, synced, or backed up to any server.
- Clearing the App's cache or uninstalling the App permanently removes all processed data from your device.

### Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-processing services. No data leaves your device.

### Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and because the App collects no data at all, no data of any user — including children — is gathered or shared.

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

## 中文版本

### 引言

Marmot Compress · 压缩大师（"本应用"）是一款以隐私为先的图片和视频压缩应用。本隐私权政策说明您使用本应用时，应用如何处理相关信息。

**简而言之：压缩大师不收集、不传输、不共享您的任何个人数据。您压缩的每一张图片和每一段视频都只保留在您的设备上，绝不会离开设备。**

### 我们收集的信息

**本应用不收集任何个人信息。** 压缩大师：

- **不**要求注册账号
- **不**在任何服务器上收集、存储或处理您的照片和视频
- **不**通过互联网发送任何数据
- **不**包含广告、统计分析、用户追踪或崩溃上报组件
- **不**向任何第三方出售或共享数据

### 应用如何使用设备权限

| 权限 | 用途 |
|---|---|
| **照片 / 媒体**（Android 13+） | 选择需要压缩的图片和视频，以及将处理后的结果保存回相册 |
| **存储**（Android 12 及以下） | 在旧版 Android 系统上读写图片和视频文件 |

所有权限仅用于提供应用的压缩和转换功能。您可以拒绝任何权限，应用将仅无法使用对应功能。

### 数据存储与安全

- 所有图片和视频处理均**在您的设备本地完成**。
- 压缩后的文件和中间结果**仅存储在您设备上本应用的私有缓存目录中**。
- 除非您主动选择覆盖，否则您的原始文件**不会**被修改。
- 任何媒体文件都不会被上传、同步或备份到任何服务器。
- 清除应用缓存或卸载应用，将永久删除您设备上的所有处理数据。

### 第三方服务

本应用不使用任何第三方分析、广告或数据处理服务。任何数据都不会离开您的设备。

### 儿童隐私

本应用并非面向 13 岁以下儿童设计；由于应用不收集任何数据，任何用户（包括儿童）的数据均不会被收集或共享。
