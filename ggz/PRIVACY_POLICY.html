引言

Veil Camera（"本应用"）是一款以隐私为先的相机应用。本隐私权政策说明您使用本应用时，应用如何处理相关信息。

简而言之：Veil Camera 不收集、不传输、不共享您的任何个人数据。应用录制的所有内容都保存在您的设备上，除非您主动导出。

我们收集的信息

本应用不收集任何个人信息。Veil Camera：

  • 不要求注册账号
  • 不在任何服务器上收集、存储或处理您的照片和视频
  • 不通过互联网发送任何数据
  • 不包含广告、统计分析、用户追踪或崩溃上报组件
  • 不向任何第三方出售或共享数据

  • 所有照片和视频仅存储在您设备上本应用的私有目录中。
  • 媒体文件不会被上传、同步或备份到任何地方。
  • 您可以将照片或视频导出到设备相册——这仅在您主动选择时发生，且导出过程完全在您的设备本地完成。
  • 在应用中删除文件，或卸载应用，将永久删除您设备上的对应数据。

第三方服务

本应用不使用任何第三方分析、广告或数据处理服务。任何数据都不会离开您的设备。

Introduction

Veil Camera ("we", "our", or "the App") is a privacy-first camera application. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it on your Android device.

The short version: Veil Camera does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data. Everything the App records stays on your device unless you explicitly export it.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Veil Camera:

  • Does not require an account or registration
  • Does not collect, store, or process your photos or videos on any server
  • Does not send any data over the internet
  • Does not include advertisements, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs
  • Does not sell or share data with third parties

How the App Uses Device Permissions

PermissionPurpose
CameraCapturing photos and videos, which is the core function of the App
MicrophoneRecording audio in your videos (optional per recording)
Photos / Media / StorageSaving photos and videos you choose to export to your device gallery

All permissions are used only to provide the App's features. You may deny any permission, and the App will simply not perform the related function.

Data Storage and Security

  • All photos and videos are stored only in the App's private storage on your device.
  • Media files are not uploaded, synced, or backed up anywhere.
  • You can export a photo or video to your device's gallery — this happens only when you explicitly choose to, and the export is performed locally on your device.
  • Deleting a file in the App, or uninstalling the App, permanently removes the corresponding data from your device.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-processing services. No data leaves your device.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and because the App collects no data at all, no data of any user — including children — is gathered or shared.
