Our Belief

Fewer features. More ease.

A good product isn't about fitting everything in. It's about caring for the few things that stay.

01

Restraint

Removing what isn't needed takes courage. We'd rather spend our time subtracting, and keep only what actually gets used.

02

Effortless

Open it and it just works — no tutorial needed. Every button's position and every tap's response is argued over.

03

Peace of mind

No ads, no tracking, no permissions we don't need. Your content belongs to you, and only to you.
