《不熄战火》隐私权政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 26 日

本政策由《不熄战火》（Fires of War）在 Google Play 商品详情中列明的开发者（“我们”）提供，适用于 Android 应用 com.warmpaw.everlightkingdom，包括内测版本。部分游戏界面使用名称“不熄战线”或“Longlight Front”。

游玩与本地存档

你可以直接游玩，无需创建账号。游戏在设备的应用专属存储空间中保存关卡进度、星章、难度、英雄选择、背包道具、剧情阅读进度，以及语言、音乐和音效设置。这些数据用于在下次打开游戏时继续游玩。我们没有接收这些存档的游戏服务器。

游戏在设备上读取屏幕尺寸等显示信息，以适配画面和按钮。游戏不读取通讯录、照片、摄像头、麦克风或精确位置。游戏不展示广告，不使用广告标识符进行追踪，也未启用 Unity Analytics 或 Unity 云端崩溃上报。我们不出售玩家个人数据，也不向广告商提供游戏存档或设备信息。

Google Play 分发和诊断

安装或更新游戏时，Google Play 会处理设备技术信息和应用版本，以提供适合设备的游戏资源。根据 Google 的说明，Play Asset Delivery 会加密传输这些信息，并在其规定的保留期后删除。Google Play 游戏资源分发说明

如果你在 Android 中选择与 Google 分享使用情况和诊断信息，Google 可能收集设备型号、系统和应用版本，以及崩溃或“应用无响应”报告。我们可以在 Play 管理中心查看 Google 提供的稳定性统计和技术报告，用来排查故障。你可以在 Android 设置中搜索“使用情况和诊断信息”来更改选择。Google 按其隐私政策处理平台数据。Google 隐私权政策

保留、备份与删除

本地存档会一直保留，直至你将其删除。在游戏的“设置”中选择“重置进度”并确认，可清除战役记录、英雄选择和背包。要同时删除语言、音效等偏好，可在 Android 的应用信息中选择“存储”并清除应用数据，或卸载游戏。这会清除本机副本。

如果设备已开启系统备份或换机迁移，Android 或设备厂商可能会备份或恢复应用数据。你可以在设备的备份设置中管理这些副本。开发者无法读取或删除你的系统备份。

联系我们或提出删除请求

如有隐私问题，或希望查询、删除你主动发给我们的信息，请发送邮件至 everflames@126.com。我们会使用你的邮箱、来信及你选择附上的资料回复并处理请求。负责处理请求的人员和提供邮件服务的供应商可能为此处理这些信息。我们仅在处理请求或履行法定义务所需期间保留相关往来，随后删除。来信无需提供游戏密码或身份证件。

Fires of War Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

This policy is provided by the developer named on the Google Play listing for Fires of War (“we”). It covers the Android app com.warmpaw.everlightkingdom, including internal test releases. Some game screens use the title “不熄战线” or “Longlight Front.”

Playing the game and saving progress

You can play without creating an account. The game saves your campaign progress, stars, difficulty, hero selection, inventory, story progress, language, music and sound preferences in the app’s private storage on your device. It uses these records to resume your game and restore your settings. We do not operate a game server that receives these saves.

The game reads display information such as screen dimensions on your device to fit the picture and controls to your screen. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location. It contains no advertising and does not use advertising identifiers to track you. Unity Analytics and Unity cloud crash reporting are disabled. We do not sell players’ personal data or provide game saves or device information to advertisers.

Google Play delivery and diagnostics

When you install or update the game, Google Play processes technical information about your device and the app version to deliver compatible game assets. Google states that Play Asset Delivery encrypts this information in transit and deletes it after a set retention period. Google Play Asset Delivery information

If you choose to share usage and diagnostics with Google in Android settings, Google may collect your device model, operating system and app versions, and crash or “app not responding” reports. We can review stability statistics and technical reports made available in Play Console to troubleshoot problems. You can change your choice by searching for “Usage & diagnostics” in Android settings. Google handles platform data under its own policy. Google Privacy Policy

Retention, backups and deletion

Local saves remain on your device until you remove them. To erase your campaign records, hero selection and inventory, open the game’s Settings, choose “Reset progress” and confirm. To also remove preferences such as language and sound, open the game’s App info in Android settings, select Storage and clear the app data, or uninstall the game. This removes the copy on that device.

If system backup or device transfer is enabled, Android or your device manufacturer may back up or restore app data. You can manage those copies in your device’s backup settings. The developer cannot access or delete your system backups.

Contact us or request deletion

For privacy questions, or to request access to or deletion of information you sent us, email us at everflames@126.com. We use your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send to respond to your request. People handling the request and our email service provider may process that information for this purpose. We keep the correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request or meet legal obligations, then delete it. You do not need to send a game password or identity document.
